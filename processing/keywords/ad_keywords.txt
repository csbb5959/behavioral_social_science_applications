# Format: one phrase per line. Lines starting with "#" and empty lines are ignored.
# Matching: lowercase the transcript, replace hyphens with spaces, drop punctuation
# except apostrophes and "%", then search each phrase as a whole phrase
# (not as a part of a longer word).
#
# Only multi-word phrases that rarely occur in normal conversation are listed.
# Single generic words ("free", "sponsor", "offer", "deal", "save") are left out on purpose,
# they would flag too much real content.
# Brand names are left out on purpose: sponsors change over the years, so a brand list
# would remove ads better in some years than in others.
